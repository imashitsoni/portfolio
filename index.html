About

Personal Overview

Greetings! I’m Ashit Soni, Founder of Blackbird IT Consultants, and a passionate creator fascinated by building meaningful digital experiences on the internet. My journey in web development began in 2017, sparked by customizing WordPress themes — an exploration that gave me strong foundations in HTML and CSS.

Over the years, my professional path has included working at a startup and later at a technology consulting agency, where I gained hands-on experience delivering scalable and user-focused solutions. After my tenure with my last two companies, I took the leap to start my own agency, Blackbird IT Consultants, focused on providing specialized Shopify development services to businesses worldwide.

Today, I combine technical expertise with strategic thinking to craft accessible, high-performing, and conversion-driven digital products for diverse clients.

Beyond the screen, I cherish spending quality time with my family and my canine companion. Exploring the vibrant streets of Indore in search of great food is one of my favorite ways to unwind and stay inspired outside the world of technology.
